ARKANSAS PATROL CHARGE TRIAGE V2.6 — FIELD SYSTEMS

THIS UPDATE COMBINES THE CURRENT FIELD-USABILITY, PROPERTY, JUVENILE, MUNICIPAL-ORDINANCE, INFOGRAPHIC, AND SPECIAL-VEHICLE WORK.

NEW PROPERTY MODULES
• Residential Burglary — includes Aggravated Residential Burglary pathways and Class A / Class Y resolution.
• Commercial Burglary — includes pharmacy consecutive-five-year enhancement.
• Breaking or Entering — listed targets, theft/felony purpose, separate-target flag.
• Unauthorized Use of Vehicle — knowing control + another person's vehicle + no owner consent; Theft cross-check.

JUVENILE AREA
• Dedicated Juvenile section on the home screen.
• Fort Smith Juvenile Curfew uses current Municode §§ 14-101—14-106.
• Covers age, location, curfew hours, all current § 14-102 defenses, parent/responsible-adult offense, business-operator offense, and waiver-permit references.

FORT SMITH ORDINANCES
• City Park Enforcement quick-reference added from current Fort Smith Municode.
• § 18-58 misdemeanor framework + common park rules.
• Applicable city ordinances surface beside Arkansas state law when the facts entered make them relevant.
• State law and city ordinance are clearly labeled separately.

UNIVERSAL REFERENCE STANDARD
• ALL criminal charge modules retain two separate controls:
    KEY TERMS — in-app bottom-sheet definitions, simplified plain English first.
    FULL STATUTE(S) — external complete controlling law.
• Both remain available during triage and on the final result.

SPECIAL VEHICLE REDESIGN
• Optional zero-experience officer lifeline: “I’m not sure what this is” and “Where do I find the specs?”
• Unresolved classification identifies the EXACT missing facts and shows facts already established.
• E-bike help explains the legally required class / top-assisted-speed / motor-wattage label under § 27-51-1704 and where to look for device/model specifications.
• Clearly states voltage is NOT wattage and observed speed is not maximum capable speed.
• Scooter help distinguishes the ≤20 mph maximum-capability definition from the >15 mph operating rule and the shared-scooter unique-ID law.
• Gas cycle help explains where to find cc and the ≤50 cc / ≤250 cc / >250 cc classification breaks.
• Slingshot/autocycle no longer dead-ends when one equipment item is NO/UNKNOWN. Baseline autocycle laws always load provisionally when appropriate.
• Autocycle equipment is checked individually: headlights, tail lamps, brakes, horn, and signal lamps.
• Electric-autocycle roadway restrictions added.
• Special Vehicle also has separate KEY TERMS / DEFINITIONS and FULL STATUTE(S) controls.
