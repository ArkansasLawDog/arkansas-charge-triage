ARKANSAS PATROL FIELD HUB V3.2.2 — FIELDING NOTES
==================================================

DEFAULT WORKFLOW
• SIMPLE MODE remains the day-one / rapid-field default.
• ADVANCED MODE adds enhancement, arrest-authority, evidence, and report support.
• Key Terms / Definitions remains separate from Full Statute / Full Law.
• A YES result is a triage aid, not a substitute for probable cause, current law,
  agency policy, supervisor direction, or prosecutor guidance.

WHAT IS CORRECTED IN V3.2.2
• Category Card Navigation
  The shared tap handler is repaired. Charge cards, Quick References, traffic
  validators, special-vehicle cards, and City Park tools now open normally.
• Navigation Audit
  All 79 registered category cards were activated in testing, including all 26
  unique Quick References and all 29 unique charge modules.

V3.2.1 MUNICIPAL-LINK CORRECTIONS RETAINED
• Fort Smith Ordinance Quick Reference
  Every source control now displays the exact ordinance number and current
  section title inside the hyperlink and opens that selected section directly.
• Fort Smith City Park Enforcement
  The offense, § 18-58 park penalty, and § 1-9 general penalty are separate,
  clearly labeled direct links. The former shared link to § 18-79 is removed.
• Fort Smith Juvenile Curfew
  Sections 14-101 through 14-106 now have separate exact source links.
• Animal Waste
  Updated from former § 4-130 to current § 4-110 following the 2023 recodification.

WHAT WAS ADDED IN V3.2
• Property Offenses > Theft of Services
  Includes dine-and-dash, lodging, transportation, labor, utility theft/diversion,
  dollar thresholds, threat routes, intent checks, arrest guidance, and reporting.
• Common Traffic > Noncommercial Driver Licensing
  Includes permit, learner, intermediate / Class DI, Class D, endorsements,
  restrictions, status codes, suspension/revocation, and fast field checks.
• Common Traffic > six additional validation tools
  Speeding; Careless / Prohibited Driving; License Status / Restrictions;
  Liability Insurance; Rear Lamps; Traffic-Control Devices / Signals.
• Infographic Library
  Eight new matching field cards; 65 cards total.

FIELDING FEEDBACK TO CAPTURE
• Screen / module and exact fact pattern entered.
• Whether the user was in Simple or Advanced Mode.
• The result shown and the result expected.
• Any question unclear on first read or any avoidable tap/scroll.
• Any missing definition, statute link, field cue, or report fact.
• Any municipal link that does not land on the section number printed in the app.
• Any category card that highlights when tapped but does not open a new screen.
• Any classification, enhancement, or arrest-authority issue.
• Any Class DI / permit / learner restriction that was hard to locate.
• Any traffic validator that returned YES when it should have stayed VERIFY.
• Any special-vehicle path that still dead-ends.
• Any infographic wording that cannot be understood at a glance.

RECOMMENDED INITIAL FIELDING
• Start with a small patrol group and include at least one newer officer.
• Run real or de-identified recent calls through both Simple and Advanced Mode.
• Compare every proposed enforcement action with current department policy and
  the linked primary law before relying on the app in the field.
• For the first Fort Smith test, open Ordinance Quick Reference > § 18-68 and
  confirm the OFFENSE link opens “Sec. 18-68. — Hours; park curfew.”
• Also open City Park Related and tap both Quick Reference cards, then open one
  charge category and confirm a randomly selected charge starts its triage.
• Record feedback by module; do not combine unrelated problems into one note.
